What is deleted, what is kept — in English

Deleted: your account record, sign-in identity, profile and avatar, contributor points, saved lists, notification inbox, direct messages to editors, your comments, your forum threads and replies, and your uploaded avatars/covers.

Kept, anonymously: articles, songs or books you published that are now part of the archive stay published but are attributed to "deleted author" — removing them would break citations elsewhere. Material kept for a legal obligation (for example the record of a copyright complaint) is retained only as long as the law requires, and you are told if that applies to you.
